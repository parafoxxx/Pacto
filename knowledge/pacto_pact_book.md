## Rules every pact follows
- A stake is charged only after a verified missed session, a pre-debit notification, and the end of the notice window.
- The Insurer receives a forfeited stake and decides on pause requests. The Insurer never stakes anything, and two people can never insure each other.
- Emergency codes: a small number per pact (we start with 4 per 12 weeks). A code protects the user's money but breaks their streak. Every code used is told to the user's circle.
- The circle is the 7-8 close people the user chose. They hear only about a charged stake, a used code, or a quit.
- Coins are funded by protein brands and can never pay a stake, be withdrawn, or be sent to another user.
